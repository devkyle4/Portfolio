Dr. Anya Korhonen

Anya Korhonen, Ph.D. — Helsinki & Zürich studio archives.

Operating at the intersection of material ecology and architectural historiography. Korhonen examines how non-extractive monolithic volumes, cast earthen elements, and acoustic voids foster contemporary rituals of contemplation.

Projects

The Grid & The Sieve

2023 — Arsenale di Venezia

Suspended monolithic partition system produced with open-pore compressed sand and volcanic ash. Interrogating urban filtration systems and acoustic absorption within monumental assembly halls.

View Project →

Sensing Soil: Terrestrial Forms

2024 — Kunsthal Rotterdam

Ram-compacted earthen chambers demonstrating ambient thermal inertia and passive humidity modulation. Formulated with excavated subsoils from Nordic railway tunnels.

View Project →

Monolithic Void & Echo

2022 — Helsinki Art Museum

Acoustic testing pavilions casting natural lime sinters and porphyry aggregate to measure human sonic perception inside windowless enclosures.
